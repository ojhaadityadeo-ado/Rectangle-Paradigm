Visual Proof: $8T_n + 1$ is a Perfect Square

Eight identical triangular staircases, each containing $T_n=1+2+\cdots+n$ unit squares, fit around one central unit square to form a square of side $2n+1$.

$$8T_n+1 =8\left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)+1 =4n(n+1)+1 =(2n+1)^2$$

Visual Rearrangement

1. One triangular staircase A staircase with rows $1,2,\ldots,n$ contains exactly $T_n$ unit squares.
2. Make eight identical copies Eight congruent staircases contribute a total area of $8T_n$ unit squares.
3. Add one unit square Place a single unit square at the center. The total area is now $8T_n+1$.
4. Rearrange without gaps Slide the eight staircases inward. Together with the center square, they exactly tile one larger square.
5. Read the side length The completed square has $n$ cells on either side of the center, plus the center cell itself: side $2n+1$. Hence its area is $(2n+1)^2$.

One staircase has rows of lengths $1,2,\ldots,n$, so its area is $T_n$.

Why the Rearrangement Proves the Identity

Area before rearranging

Each colored staircase has rows containing $1,2,\ldots,n$ unit squares, so each has area

$$T_n=1+2+\cdots+n=\frac{n(n+1)}2.$$

Eight copies plus the single center square therefore have total area $8T_n+1$.

Area after rearranging

No cells are added, removed, stretched, or overlapped. The same pieces form a square with side

$$n+1+n=2n+1.$$

Hence the same area is $(2n+1)^2$. Therefore

$$\boxed{8T_n+1=(2n+1)^2}.$$
