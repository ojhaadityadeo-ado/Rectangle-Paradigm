Definitions:

Average ($S$) = $\frac{x+y}{2}$

Difference ($D$) = $\frac{x-y}{2}$

The Geometric Insight

Every odd composite number $N$ can be factored into two odd integers $x$ and $y$ such that $N = x \times y$ (where $x > y \ge 1$). This algebraically forms a rectangle of area $N$.

Because $x$ and $y$ are both odd, their sum ($x+y$) and their difference ($x-y$) are always even numbers. This guarantees that the average $S$ and the distance $D$ are perfect integers.

The Transformation

Notice that the original width is exactly $x = S + D$ and the height is exactly $y = S - D$.

If we split the rectangle vertically into a left block of width $S$ and a right block of width $D$, we can detach the right block.

By rotating this right block $90^\circ$ and placing it at the bottom, the pieces perfectly bound a large square of side $S$, missing exactly a small square of side $D$ in the corner!

$$ x \cdot y = \left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)^2 = S^2 - D^2 $$
