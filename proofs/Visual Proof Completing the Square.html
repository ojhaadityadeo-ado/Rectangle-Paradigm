The Geometric Process:

  1. Start with the Expression: We begin with the area $x^2 + ax$, represented by a large blue square of side $x$ and an adjacent green rectangle of dimensions $x$ by $a$.
  2. Halve the Coefficient: We symmetrically split the green $ax$ rectangle into two equal halves, each with a width of $\frac{a}{2}$. The total area remains exactly the same.
  3. Relocate Rectangles: We keep one half attached to the right side of the $x^2$ square, and swing the other half to attach to the bottom edge.
  4. Identify the Deficit: The resulting shape is almost a large square of side $(x + \frac{a}{2})$, but a small corner piece is missing! The dimensions of this missing gap are $\frac{a}{2}$ by $\frac{a}{2}$. Adding this missing $(\frac{a}{2})^2$ piece "completes the square".
$$x^2 + ax + \left(\frac{a}{2}\right)^2 = \left(x + \frac{a}{2}\right)^2$$
