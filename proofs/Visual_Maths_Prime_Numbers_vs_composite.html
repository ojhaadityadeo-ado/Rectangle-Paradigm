Prime or Composite? Discover with Rectangles

Choose a number and arrange its unit tiles into every possible whole-number rectangle. More than one rectangle shape means composite; only 1 × p means prime.

Prime vs Composite Numbers

Use tiles to build exact rectangles. If the only rectangle is \(1\times p\), the number is prime. If another whole-number rectangle is possible, the number is composite.

What is happening?
We try to arrange all 17 tiles into exact rectangles. Only 1 row of 17 works, so 17 is prime.

Big ideas for kids

Prime means “only one rectangle shape”. A prime number can only make the \(1\times p\) rectangle using whole-number side lengths.
Composite means “more rectangle shapes are possible”. For example, 12 can make \(1\times12\), \(2\times6\), and \(3\times4\).
