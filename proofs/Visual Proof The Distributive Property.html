Visual Proof: The Distributive Property

5 × (6 + 4) = (5 × 6) + (5 × 4)   →   50 = 30 + 20
a=5
b=6
$ab$ = 30
c=4
$ac$ = 20

How to understand this proof:

Imagine a large rectangle. The height of this rectangle is $a$, and its total width is made up of two pieces: $b$ and $c$. Because the total width is $(b + c)$, the total area of the large rectangle is calculated as $a \times (b + c)$.

Now, look at the visual above. We can easily split this large rectangle into two smaller distinct rectangles:

  • The Blue Rectangle: Its height is $a$ and its width is $b$. Therefore, its area is $ab$.
  • The Green Rectangle: Its height is $a$ and its width is $c$. Therefore, its area is $ac$.

Since these two smaller rectangles combine perfectly to form the large rectangle, the area of the large rectangle must be exactly equal to the sum of the areas of the two smaller rectangles. Therefore, we arrive at the distributive property:

$$a(b + c) = ab + ac$$
