Fraction Division: How Many Small Pieces Fit?

To understand \(\frac34\div\frac18\), ask a simple question: “How many pieces of size \(\frac18\) fit inside \(\frac34\)?” The bar lets us see and count those pieces.

\(\frac34=\frac68\), so six \(\frac18\)-pieces fit.

How to explain fraction division to small kids

Think “How many fit?” \(\frac34\div\frac18\) means: “How many pieces of size \(\frac18\) fit inside \(\frac34\)?”
Draw the starting amount. Shade \(\frac34\) of one whole bar.
Cut the whole into eighths. When the bar is divided into 8 equal pieces, \(\frac34\) covers 6 of them. So \[ \frac34=\frac68. \]
Count the eighths. There are 6 pieces of size \(\frac18\) inside \(\frac34\).
So the division answer is 6. \[ \boxed{\frac34\div\frac18=6}. \]
