Visual Proof: Area of a Trapezium

Step: 0 / 5

1. Draw the Trapezium: Draw a trapezium with horizontal bases $a$ (top) and $b$ (bottom), and a vertical height $h$.

Connecting the Geometry to the Math

Now, look at the dimensions of this newly formed rectangle at the end of the animation:

  • Height of the Rectangle: Since we cut the original trapezium across its middle, the new height is $\frac{h}{2}$.
  • Length of the Rectangle: The bottom edge now completely combines the original bases, giving a total length equal to $(a + b)$.

Because we only sliced and rearranged the pieces without adding or removing any area, the Area of the Trapezium is identical to the Area of this new Rectangle:

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Area} &= \text{Length} \times \text{Height} \\ &= (a + b) \times \frac{h}{2} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}(a + b) \cdot h \end{aligned}$$
