Deriving the Inequality

We start with a unit square of area $1$. We divide its side lengths proportionally into segments $\frac{a}{a+b}$ and $\frac{b}{a+b}$.

By doing this, we can fit 4 congruent rectangles inside the unit square in a pinwheel formation. The area of each rectangle is simply its width times its height:

$$ \text{Area} = \left(\frac{a}{a+b}\right) \times \left(\frac{b}{a+b}\right) = \frac{ab}{(a+b)^2} $$

Comparing Areas

Because all 4 rectangles fit comfortably inside the unit square (leaving a central gap), their combined area must be less than or equal to $1$:

$$ 4 \cdot \frac{ab}{(a+b)^2} \le 1 $$

Multiply both sides by $ab$:

$$ \frac{4a^2b^2}{(a+b)^2} \le ab $$

Taking the square root of both sides perfectly separates the Geometric and Harmonic means!

$$ \frac{2ab}{a+b} \le \sqrt{ab} $$
