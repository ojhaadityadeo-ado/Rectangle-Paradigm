Average Means “Same Total Amount, Equal Height”

Unequal columns can be cut and their extra parts moved to shorter columns. Nothing is added and nothing is removed. When all columns become the same height, that common height is the mean.

\(\text{average}=\frac{2+7+4+5+2}{5}=4\)

Average calculation
\(\frac{2+7+4+5+2}{5}=4\)
What is happening?
Some columns are too tall and some are too short. We will move only the extra area from tall columns to short ones.
Quick facts
Total amount 20
Number of columns 5
Equal height 4
Total after sharing 20
Same total area \(20\), spread equally across \(5\) columns, gives height \(4\).

How to explain average to small kids

Start with unequal amounts. Imagine each column is a stack of blocks. Some children have more blocks and some have fewer.
Do not change the total. We are only moving blocks from one column to another. No block disappears, and no new block appears.
Share the extra fairly. Cut the extra part from columns above the average line and give it to columns below the line.
Stop when every height is equal. Once every column has the same height, that height is the average.
Why the formula works. If the total amount is \(T\) and there are \(n\) equal columns, then each column must have height \[ \frac{T}{n}. \]
Average = same total amount, shared equally.
