Division as Making Equal Rows

Choose how many rows you want. The tiles will try to sit in equal rows. If every row gets the same number, division is exact. If not, some tiles are left over.

Choose number of rows 6 rows
24 tiles into 6 rows Each row gets 4 tiles.
Division sentence
24 ÷ 6 = 4
What is happening?
We are sharing 24 tiles into 6 equal rows. Each row gets 4, so 24 ÷ 6 = 4.

Factors as Rectangle Arrays

Every factor pair makes a perfect rectangle with no tiles left over. So factors are really just all the different exact rectangles the number can build.

Factor idea
We are checking all rectangle shapes that can be made with 24 tiles exactly.
Factor sentence
24 = 1×24 = 2×12 = 3×8 = 4×6
What is happening?
A factor pair is a pair of whole numbers that multiplies to make the total number of tiles.

Big ideas for kids

Division means making equal groups or equal rows. If 24 tiles are arranged into 6 equal rows, then each row has 4 tiles.
Sometimes there is a remainder. If the tiles cannot fit equally, the extra tiles are called “left over” or “remainder”.
Factors are the exact row arrangements. A factor pair makes a rectangle with no leftovers at all.
Division and factors are connected. If 24 ÷ 6 = 4, then 6 and 4 are factors of 24.
Rectangles help us see the maths. Instead of only reading a number sentence, we can see what the numbers are doing.
