Visual Proof of Area Identity

$$ax - by = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)(x - y) + \frac{1}{2}(a - b)(x + y)$$

Adjust sliders to configure the geometry, or press Step-by-Step Animation to begin the visual proof.

How to understand this proof:

  1. Start with the target area: We start with a large blue rectangle with dimensions $a$ by $x$, giving it a total area of $ax$.
  2. Remove the corner: We want to subtract the red corner area ($by$). What remains is an L-shape that has exactly the area of $ax - by$.
  3. Partition the L-Shape: By cutting the L-shape diagonally, we split it into two separate trapezoids.
  4. Duplicate and Rotate: To easily find the area of these trapezoidal shapes, we duplicate them. Rotating identical trapezoids and snapping them together forms perfect rectangles!
  5. The Final Result: The top shape becomes a rectangle of width $(a+b)$ and height $(x-y)$. The left shape becomes a rectangle of width $(a-b)$ and height $(x+y)$. Because we duplicated the pieces to make these rectangles, our original area ($ax - by$) is exactly half of their combined area.
$$ax - by = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)(x - y) + \frac{1}{2}(a - b)(x + y)$$
