Product of Four Numbers in A.P.

A visual proof that the product of four positive numbers in an arithmetic progression is always a difference of two squares.

$$ a(a + d)(a + 2d)(a + 3d) = (a^2 + 3ad + d^2)^2 - (d^2)^2 $$
Base a 2.0
Difference d 1.0
Step: 0 / 5

Proof Progression

1. The First Identity The entire rectangle has a width of $(a+d)(a+2d)$. This region can be split into a main area of size $a(a + 3d)$ plus two extra vertical strips, each with an area of $d^2$.
2. Interpreting the Product The total product $P = a(a + d)(a + 2d)(a + 3d)$ can be grouped as $[a(a + 3d)] \times [(a + d)(a + 2d)]$. Substituting our identity, the current rectangle's total area exactly represents this product!
3. Geometric Rearrangement By taking the second $d^2$ strip, rotating it, and placing it on top of the main block, we form an "L-Shape". Both outer dimensions are now perfectly symmetric: $a^2 + 3ad + d^2$.
4. The Missing Corner Our rearranged L-shape represents our total product, but it forms an almost-complete larger square. The missing top-right corner is a small square with area $(d^2)^2$.
5. The Bounding Square We construct the complete bounding square around the L-shape. Its side length is exactly $a^2 + 3ad + d^2$.
6. Conclusion Therefore, the total product (the shaded L-shape) is exactly equal to the area of the large outer bounding square minus the area of the small missing corner.

Algebraic Derivation

1. Evaluating the Inner Terms

Consider the product of the four consecutive terms in arithmetic progression: $$ P = a(a+d)(a+2d)(a+3d) $$

Group the inner two terms and expand them:

$$ (a+d)(a+2d) = a^2 + 3ad + 2d^2 $$

Notice that the outer two terms multiply to $a(a+3d) = a^2+3ad$. This means the inner product is exactly $2d^2$ larger than the outer product.

2. Forming the Square

Let $X = a(a+3d) = a^2+3ad$. The entire product becomes:

$$ P = X(X + 2d^2) = X^2 + 2Xd^2 $$

This is the area of the L-shape in our animation! By completing the square (adding and subtracting the missing corner $d^4$), we get: $$ P = (X^2 + 2Xd^2 + d^4) - d^4 = (X+d^2)^2 - (d^2)^2 $$ Substituting $X$ back confirms the final identity.
